For businesses and individuals

Tell the firm who you are contacting them for.

Whether you represent a business or need help with a personal matter, start with a short enquiry and ask whether the firm handles your issue.

B

For businesses

Business legal enquiries

Explain the business context and the question or document involved. Ask whether the firm can advise on that kind of issue, who would handle it and how a consultation is arranged.

  • Share a brief summary of the matter
  • Ask about scope, fees and appointment timing
  • Confirm the firm's current availability

For individuals and families

Personal matter enquiries

Give a concise description of what you need help with. The firm can tell you whether the matter is within its current practice and what a first conversation involves.

  • Say what type of matter you are enquiring about
  • Ask who will speak with you and what to prepare
  • Confirm the fee and next available time
