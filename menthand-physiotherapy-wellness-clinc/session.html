Your session • What to expect

Know what happens at your first physio visit.

A first conversation is a chance to explain what feels difficult, ask questions and understand the proposed next step before treatment begins.

Ask about booking ↗Call the clinic

A clear, respectful process

One visit, four useful steps.

Session details depend on your needs. Ask the physiotherapist to explain each part and let them know if you feel discomfort or need a pause.

01

Talk through your concern

Share when it started, what makes it better or worse, and what you hope to do more comfortably.

02

Understand the assessment

The clinician may ask questions and check movement or function. You can ask why a check is needed.

03

Discuss options

Ask about the suggested approach, what it involves, any risks and possible alternatives.

04

Agree the next step

Discuss home exercises, follow-up, fees and how progress will be reviewed before you decide.

Before you arrive

Make your first visit easier.

01 Confirm the appointment time with the clinic by phone or WhatsApp.

02 Wear comfortable clothing that allows easy movement.

03 Bring relevant referral notes, reports or questions if you have them.

04 Tell the therapist about pain, health conditions or movement concerns before starting.

Not sure if physio can help?

Tell us what is limiting you.

Ask the team whether a physiotherapy assessment may be appropriate. If symptoms are sudden or severe, seek urgent medical care.
