The clinic

A calm, clean clinic in the heart of Ushafa

TK DENTAL CLINIC is at B2 Ushafa Plaza, opposite Santos Resort, Ushafa, Bwari. We built the clinic around one simple idea: nobody should dread the dentist.

About us

Founded and led by Mrs James O.K.

TK DENTAL CLINIC is a women-owned clinic in Ushafa, founded by Mrs James O.K., who leads a small, caring team. We take time with every patient, especially first-timers and anyone who feels anxious about dental visits.

The clinic is equipped with modern dental technology and digital X-rays for precise diagnoses with lower radiation exposure. From the waiting area to the dental chair, everything here is designed to help you relax: a quiet room, honest explanations, gentle hands, and a team that listens before it treats.

Your first visit

What to expect, step by step

If you have not visited a dentist in a while, this is exactly how your first appointment with us goes.

1

A warm welcome and a real conversation

We sit down with you first. Tell us your dental history, what hurts, what worries you, and what you want from your visit. Nothing happens until you are ready.

2

A gentle check-up

We examine your teeth and gums carefully, take digital images only if needed, and show you anything we find on screen so you can see it too.

3

A clear plan, in plain English

You leave knowing exactly what treatment you need, what it involves, what it costs, and when we can do it. You decide. No pressure.

Hygiene & safety

Cleanliness you can see

We know many patients worry about infection control. Here is exactly what we do, every single day.

  • Instruments are sterilised after every patient
  • Single-use items wherever possible
  • Gloves, masks and protective barriers for every procedure
  • Surfaces and chairs cleaned between every appointment
