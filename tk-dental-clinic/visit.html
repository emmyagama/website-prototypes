Good to know

Before you come

Booking beats walk-ins

Walk-ins are welcome when we can, but a WhatsApp message helps us reserve the right amount of time for you.

Home visits available

If you cannot easily come to the clinic, we also offer home dental services. Ask us on WhatsApp to arrange one.

Bring your medication list

If you take any regular medication or have allergies, bring the list along so we can plan your treatment safely.

Something hurting?

Call 0810 877 8310 and describe your symptoms. We will tell you honestly how soon we can see you.
