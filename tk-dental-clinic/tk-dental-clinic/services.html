Our services

Gentle treatments for the whole family

Every treatment at TK DENTAL CLINIC starts with a conversation. We check carefully, explain your options in plain English, and only proceed when you are ready. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is a surprise.

Check-up & Consultation

A full look at your teeth and gums, an honest conversation about what you need, and a clear plan. The right first step, whether it has been months or years since your last visit.

Book a check-up

Teeth Cleaning & Polishing

Also called scaling and polishing. We remove plaque, tartar and surface stains that brushing misses, then polish your teeth smooth. Most people benefit from a clean every 6 months, and it is the easiest way to prevent bigger problems later.

Book a cleaning

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening that lifts stains from coffee, tea and age, safely and comfortably. You leave with a visibly brighter smile, and we show you how to keep it that way.

Book whitening

Fillings, Crowns & Bridges

For cavities and damaged teeth, we place tooth-coloured fillings, and restore broken or missing teeth with crowns and bridges that look and feel natural. The area is numbed properly, so the procedure is comfortable.

Book a consult

Root Canal Treatment

A root canal saves a badly infected tooth instead of removing it. We numb the tooth thoroughly, clean the canals carefully, and seal the tooth so it can stay in your mouth comfortably for years.

Book a consult

Braces & Clear Aligners

Corrective braces and clear aligners gradually straighten crooked or crowded teeth. We plan every orthodontic treatment around your bite, not just the front view.

Book a consult

Tooth Extraction

When a tooth is too damaged to save, we remove it as gently as possible, explain exactly how to care for the area while it heals, and check on you afterwards.

Book extraction

We also offer dental implants, veneers, dentures, gum (periodontal) treatment, and home visits for patients who cannot easily come to the clinic. Ask us on WhatsApp what is right for you.

In pain right now?

Call us on 0810 877 8310 and describe your symptoms. We will tell you honestly how soon we can see you. If the pain is severe, or you have swelling and fever, seek emergency medical care straight away.

Not sure what you need?

Start with a check-up

A full mouth examination takes a short visit. We will show you what we see, tell you what matters now and what can wait, and you decide from there. No pressure, no upselling.
