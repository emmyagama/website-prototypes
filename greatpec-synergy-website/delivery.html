Delivery and payment on delivery

Same-day Abuja delivery and payment on delivery appeared on the older shop. Confirm whether either is still offered for your area, item, timing and delivery fee.

  • Ask for delivery cost and timing in writing.
  • Do not assume other cities are covered.

Authenticity and warranty

Ask for proof you can verify.

Brand names alone are not enough. Check the actual product and confirm warranty coverage for its serial number and purchase region.

Inspect the unit

  • Match the model name and serial label on the unit and box.
  • Check included cables, ports, display and accessories.
  • Ask for a receipt and the warranty terms for this unit.
