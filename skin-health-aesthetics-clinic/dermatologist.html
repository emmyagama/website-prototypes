A beginner-friendly approach

Skin care should feel clear, not complicated.

Tell the clinic about the concern, what you have already tried and what you hope to learn. If you are considering an aesthetic treatment, ask who will carry it out, what it involves and what aftercare may be needed.

There is no need to decide on a procedure in a WhatsApp chat. Use the first conversation to confirm whether a consultation is suitable and what it will cost.
