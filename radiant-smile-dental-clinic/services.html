Our services

Complete dental care, explained in plain English

Every treatment at Radiant Smile Dental Clinic starts with a conversation. We check carefully, explain your options, and only go ahead when you are ready. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is a surprise.

Root Canal Treatment

Relieve the pain of an infected tooth and protect your natural tooth. We numb thoroughly, clean the canals carefully and seal the tooth so it can stay in your mouth for years.

Book root canal treatment

Scaling & Polishing

A professional clean that removes plaque, tartar and surface stains that brushing misses, then polishes your teeth smooth. Most people benefit from a clean every 6 months.

Book scaling and polishing

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening that lifts stains safely and comfortably. We explain what to expect first, and show you how to keep your results.

Book teeth whitening

Tooth Filling

For cavities and damaged teeth, we place fillings that repair the tooth and blend with your natural smile. The area is numbed properly so the procedure is comfortable.

Book tooth filling

Tooth Extraction

When a tooth is too damaged to save, we remove it as gently as possible, explain how to care for the area while it heals, and check on you afterwards.

Book tooth extraction

Braces

Braces gradually align crooked or crowded teeth. We plan your treatment around your bite, not just the front view, and keep you informed at every visit.

Book braces

Veneers

Thin, carefully designed veneers can improve the look of worn, chipped or uneven teeth. We talk through whether they suit your teeth before anything is decided.

Book veneers

Jaw Fracture Repair

Professional care for jaw injuries and fractures. If you have had an injury to your face or jaw, call us first so we can advise you on the next step.

Book jaw fracture repair

Prices depend on your individual needs, so we give you a clear quote after your examination, not before. Ask us on WhatsApp if you want to know what a treatment usually involves.

In pain right now?

Call us on 0916 519 6388 and describe your symptoms. We will tell you honestly how soon we can see you. If the pain is severe, or you have swelling and fever, seek emergency medical care straight away.

Not sure what you need?

Start with a consultation

A full examination takes a short visit. We will show you what we see, tell you what matters now and what can wait, and you decide from there. No pressure.
