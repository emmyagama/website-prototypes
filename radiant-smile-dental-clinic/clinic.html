The clinic

A calm, neat clinic at Metta Mall, Kubwa

Radiant Smile Dental Clinic is on Arab Road in Kubwa, Abuja. We built the clinic around one simple idea: a dental visit should feel calm, clean and well explained.

About us

High-class dentistry, with attention to detail

Our team takes time with every patient, especially first-timers and anyone who feels anxious about the dentist. Many patients tell us about the friendly staff, the neat surroundings and the way each step is explained.

From the reception to the treatment room, the clinic is kept tidy and calm, so you can focus on your visit instead of the surroundings.

Your first visit

What to expect, step by step

If you have not visited a dentist in a while, this is how your first appointment goes.

1

A warm welcome and a real conversation

We sit down with you first. Tell us your dental history, what hurts, what worries you, and what you want from your visit. Nothing happens until you are ready.

2

A gentle examination

We examine your teeth and gums carefully and show you anything we find, so you can see it too.

3

A clear plan, in plain English

You leave knowing what treatment you need, what it involves, and when we can do it. You decide. No pressure.

Hygiene & safety

Cleanliness you can see

Many patients worry about infection control. Here is what we do for every patient.

  • Instruments are sterilised after every patient
  • Single-use items wherever possible
  • Gloves, masks and protective barriers for every procedure
  • Surfaces and chairs cleaned between every appointment
